# AI-Based Early Detection of Students at Academic Risk

An AI-assisted academic support platform designed to help teachers identify emerging student risk signals early, understand the contributing factors, provide targeted learning support, communicate with parents, and track progress over time.

This project was developed as a prototype for the **Horizon 2026** hackathon under the theme **AI with Education**.

---

## 1. Problem Statement

### AI-Based Early Detection of Students at Academic Risk

Students may experience declining academic performance because of multiple interacting factors such as:

- Declining attendance
- Falling assessment performance
- Missed assignments
- Weak subject performance
- Declining engagement
- Lack of timely academic support

In many learning environments, these signals may be visible separately but are not always combined into one actionable view.

The objective of this project is to provide an early-warning and intervention workflow that helps educators identify students who may need additional academic support.
