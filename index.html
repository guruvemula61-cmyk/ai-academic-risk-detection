The Problem

Academic risk often develops gradually.

A single examination result may show the outcome, but it may not show when the underlying academic pattern started changing.

Turning scattered signals into an understandable story.

Teachers may observe changes across attendance, assessments, assignments, and engagement at different points in time.

This prototype brings those signals together into one explainable early-warning workflow.

Explainable Risk

A score is useful only when the reason is understandable.

The system connects the risk score with observable academic signals so teachers can review the underlying pattern.

Example Risk Profile

A student may be flagged because recent attendance has declined, quiz performance is trending downward, and assignments have been missed.

Plain-Language Explanation

“Attendance has declined during recent weeks and quiz performance is also showing a downward trend. The recent assignment gap is contributing additional risk.”


The teacher can then review the student's context before deciding whether additional academic support is appropriate.

Intervention

Detection should lead to an actionable next step.

The intervention screen connects risk information with practical teacher-led support.

Revision Plan

Generate a focused academic support plan based on the student's observed risk factors.

  • Review recent weak areas
  • Assign targeted revision
  • Monitor the next assessment

Telugu Parent Communication

Provide a clear Telugu communication script that teachers can review before speaking with parents.

  • Explain the observed change
  • Suggest supportive action
  • Encourage collaboration

Teacher Checklist

Keep intervention actions visible and track completion inside the prototype.

  • Talk to student
  • Call parent
  • Assign revision plan
  • Mark action done

Responsible AI

Built for support, not automatic judgment.

The prototype keeps teachers in the decision loop and clearly separates demonstration data from real student records.

Human Oversight

Risk information is intended to support teacher review, not replace professional judgment.

Explainability

The prototype shows contributing signals alongside the risk score instead of presenting a prediction alone.

Synthetic Data

The demonstration uses synthetic student records and does not require real student personal information.

Support-Oriented Language

The interface uses “Needs Support” rather than stigmatizing students with failure labels.

No High-Stakes Automation

The prototype does not make final academic decisions automatically.

Prototype Transparency

Synthetic recovery examples and prototype voice features are clearly identified as demonstrations.
