The Problem

Academic difficulty rarely appears as a single signal.

Attendance, assessment performance and assignment completion can change at different times. A support system needs to bring these indicators together so educators can review the emerging pattern and respond appropriately.

Attendance Decline A student's attendance may gradually fall before academic performance visibly changes.
Assessment Decline Falling quiz or assessment performance can indicate that additional academic support may be useful.
Missed Assignments Repeatedly incomplete work provides another signal for teacher review.
Delayed Intervention When signals are reviewed separately, emerging support needs may be harder to identify.
